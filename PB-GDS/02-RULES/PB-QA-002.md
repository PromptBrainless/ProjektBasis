# PB-QA-002 — Evaluate prompts against explicit acceptance criteria

- type: QUALITY
- source: PB-GDS-SRC-007

## Agent behavior

For reusable AI artifacts define at least:
1. task success criteria
2. unacceptable behavior
3. representative inputs
4. expected characteristics of outputs
5. regression cases when practical

Evaluation may be qualitative or automated, but the method must be stated.

## Prohibited behavior

Do not infer prompt quality from a single successful generation.
